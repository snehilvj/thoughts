Welcome to *thoughts*  - my little corner of the internet.

I'm Snehil, a software engineer and startup founder. I've spent years building products, writing code, and figuring things out the hard way. This blog is where I put all of that into words.

## What to expect here

This isn't a purely technical blog. Sure, I'll write about engineering  - deep dives into tools I use, architectural decisions I've made, and the occasional tutorial. But I'll also write about:

- **The startup journey**  - the unglamorous reality of building something from scratch
- **Opinions**  - hot takes and cold takes on the tech industry
- **Life stuff**  - travel, food, experiences that shape how I think

I believe the best engineers are well-rounded people, and the best writing comes from connecting ideas across domains. A lesson from a failed deployment can mirror a lesson from a road trip gone wrong.

## Why write publicly?

Mostly for myself. Writing forces clarity. When you have to explain something to a stranger on the internet, you realize how little you actually understood it.

But also  - I've learned so much from other people putting their thoughts out there. Blog posts from random engineers have saved me hours of debugging, changed my perspective on problems, and introduced me to ideas I never would have found otherwise.

This is my contribution to that tradition.

Let's see where this goes.
